    Try it

    Listening hears everything playing on the chosen device, not only Steam. Tell everyone in the voice chat before you start.

    First-time setup

    1. Install VB-CABLE from VB-Audio and restart Windows if it asks.
    2. Open Voice settings. Add your AI key under AI & personality (a Google AI key for Gemini Live).
    3. Under Voice, choose CABLE Input for “Tyler speaks into” and your headset or speakers for “Tyler listens to”.
    4. In Steam, open Settings → Voice and choose CABLE Output as your microphone.
    5. Join a group voice chat and press Play test tone into Steam. Ask a friend whether they heard it.
    6. Tell everyone Tyler is listening, tick the consent box in Voice settings, then press Start voice.

    To talk yourself as well, route your microphone into the same cable with a mixer such as Voicemeeter. The app never controls or intercepts Steam's input.

    What runs where

    Steam text chat
    The Steam chat window in this app: replies, commands, trivia, arcade games, party host, votes, moderation (only what the bot's Steam account is allowed to do) and the soundboard's chat triggers.
    Steam voice
    This window: Standard voice or Gemini Live through the Windows audio helper and a virtual cable, with the Steam desktop client.
    Chrome extension
    The same features in Steam web chat, plus things only a browser tab can do: playing soundboard clips and YouTube music into web voice chat, and screen-aware comments from a shared screen.

    Both use the same settings design, personality rules and engines. Your keys and memory stay on this computer.
