ROBOTIC TYLER v2.27.0 — patch notes

Five new themes (25 in all), each with its own Tyler logo, status strip and extras, on every surface:
the chat panel, compact mode, Settings, the Voice Studio, the desktop window and the phone remote.

Rust
- Dark see-through panels, condensed capitals, a rust-red line, a hazard stripe and rivets, and tabs like inventory slots.
- Vitals: health = connection, calories = spending cap left, hydration = reply speed. Status effects (privilege, comfort, crafting, radio, raid alarm, bleeding) show while they apply.
- The belt: six clips, keys 1–6, with pickup notices.
- Crafting (Q): real actions as blueprints, with a queue.
- Tool Cupboard: Stay connected's upkeep.
- Wounded, then dead: Help up (full check) and Respawn (Connect & start).

Xbox 360 (2005)
- Silver blades in each tab's colour, glossy green selections and pill notices.
- Ring of Light: Tyler plus friends in the call. It spins while he writes, and shows three red lights on a problem (click to check).
- Gamer card: gamerscore, rep and 10 achievements from real use.
- The Guide (G). A blade sweeps across when you change tab. Notices for sign-in and party.

iPod classic
- White screen in black glass, the title bar with play sign and battery, blue selection strips.
- The status strip is Now Playing.
- Click wheel: drag or scroll to scroll, MENU (M), ⏮ ⏭ for tabs, ▶❙❙ to pause or start, the centre for Now Playing.
- The iPod menu, Now Playing with the Music DJ's song, and Cover Flow of your clips.
- The start-up screen with the Apple logo when Tyler starts. Compact mode is the whole iPod.

MSN Messenger
- Sky-blue gloss, and a display-picture frame in the presence colour.
- Presence: Online, On the Phone, Busy, Away. "is typing a message…" and What I'm listening to.
- Contact list of Tyler's parts. Nudge (test beep + shake), Emoticons (clips), and the status menu on the display picture.
- Corner pop-ups with the butterfly.

PlayStation 3 (XMB)
- A violet wave whose tint follows Tyler's state, thin white type, and the category row (← →).
- The clock. "Signed in to PlayStation Network." when Tyler starts.
- Options (O), trophies (bronze/silver/gold, level) and Information with the PlayStation logo.

Logos and artwork
- The logos are the owner's artwork, mirrored to face like the other 20, with their text and symbols turned back to read correctly (the two Messenger buddies stay mirrored).
- Closer crops at small sizes.
- The butterfly, PlayStation logo and Apple logo are cut from the owner's backgrounds and built in, like the logos. The backgrounds aren't shown.
- The phone remote and Settings → Appearance list all 25 themes.

Fixed
- Turning the phone remote off no longer stalls while a phone is connected (its live stream reconnected and the server waited for it).

Tested
- Chromium with the panel preview and a mocked Steam page (no real Steam, Windows audio or Gemini Live): all 25 themes switch live; the new themes' extras, menus and keys, and the three brand marks are drawn.
- Settings → Appearance with 25 previews.
- The phone remote with all 25 themes (real server, iPhone-sized Chromium).
- Unit: theme lists, files on all surfaces, 4.5:1 contrast.
- Real Electron (Linux) with a mock Steam page: the desktop check passes. The remote tests pass eight runs in a row after the stop fix.
- Not tested: the new themes on the real Steam page, in the Windows app and on a real phone.
