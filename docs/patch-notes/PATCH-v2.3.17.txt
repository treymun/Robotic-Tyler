ROBOTIC TYLER v2.3.17 - Louder independent music volume

UPDATE
Close Steam web chat tabs, replace all contents of the SAME existing
unpacked browser-extension folder, and Reload at chrome://extensions.
Reopen Steam chat, Connect & start and share the music tab audio again.
Keep the original folder/extension ID to preserve settings and keys.

VOLUME
The music relay no longer passes through the AI bridge's 35% master gain.
It now has an independent 0-200% gain range, a visible percentage label,
smooth volume changes and a peak compressor (threshold -3 dB, ratio 20:1).
AI speech and soundboard volumes are unchanged. The compressor helps with
peaks but does not guarantee distortion-free output for every source/mix.

Commands:
music volume 50  - quieter starting point
music volume 100 - source level before peak compression
music volume 150 - boost
music volume 200 - maximum boost
music volume 0   - mute

Because the old extra attenuation is removed, the same slider percentage
is louder than before. Start at 50 and adjust. Also check YouTube's player
volume and the listener's Steam volume for ROBOTIC TYLER. This is music-only
volume, not a change to your system's playback or your friends' computers.

ADS
This patch does not block or skip ads. YouTube Premium Lite can still show
ads on music. Full YouTube Premium includes ad-free music playback when
the correct subscribed account is signed into the music tab. Embedded
creator promotions or other content are not removed by this extension.
https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-premium-lite-background-play-downloads/
No subscription purchase or account change is performed by this update.

All prior features remain included. No keys/settings are cleared. Chrome
music tab control and Steam audio delivery remain experimental and have
not been tested live here. 82 automated checks and syntax checks pass,
including actual mixer-node wiring with a simulated AudioContext, gain
clamping, mute, compressor output, independent AI gain and disconnect.
