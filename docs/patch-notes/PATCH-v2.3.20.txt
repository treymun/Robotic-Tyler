ROBOTIC TYLER v2.3.20 - Music queue clear/reset

music clear / !music clear
Remove every waiting song while the current song continues. Current skip
votes remain unchanged. A short confirmation is posted in Steam chat.

music reset / !music reset
Stop music and clear waiting songs, skip votes and pending local music
commands/events. YouTube audio sharing stays ready for new requests.
Fresh requests after reset completes work normally. This does not block
future spam; it clears the current queue.

Matching Clear waiting queue and Reset music buttons are in the Music panel.
No manually entered Steam IDs are needed for either command. The existing
two-different-account skip rule, fair queue, names and chat cards remain.

INSTALL
Close Steam chat tabs. Copy ALL browser-extension contents over the SAME
existing unpacked extension folder, including neural. Reload the existing
extension at chrome://extensions. Reopen chat, Connect & start and re-share
the YouTube tab audio. Do not uninstall or change folders; settings stay.
All previous features and patches included; Electron/native unchanged.

Tests cover clearing a full personal queue without stopping the current
song, preserving its votes, reset clearing votes, stale player events,
fresh requests after reset and panel buttons. Live Steam playback was not
operated during these tests.
