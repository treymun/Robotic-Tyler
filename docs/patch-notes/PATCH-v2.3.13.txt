ROBOTIC TYLER v2.3.13 - Bundled local Silero AI speech detection

UPDATE WITHOUT LOSING SETTINGS
1. Close every Steam web chat tab.
2. Back up your existing unpacked extension folder.
3. Extract this ZIP. Copy ALL contents of ROBOTIC_TYLER_v2.3/browser-extension
   over the SAME existing extension folder, including the new neural folder.
4. Reload the EXISTING extension at chrome://extensions. Do not uninstall
   or use Load unpacked from a different path. Keep the extension ID.
5. Reopen Steam chat, join voice and use Connect & start.
6. Conversation > Fine-tune listening & turn-taking: enable
   AI speech detection - ignore keyboard-only noise.

WHAT CHANGED
The keyboardFilter preference now enables actual Silero VAD v5 neural
speech classification through ONNX Runtime Web 1.22.0. Both model and
runtime are bundled locally using the archives supplied by the user.
There is no extra application, live CDN download, subscription or extra
audio upload for this local classification. Accepted speech still uses
the configured transcription/AI provider, as before.

Incoming remote Steam audio is recorded as a candidate clip. After the
quiet gap, the audio is converted to 16-kHz PCM and checked locally in an
extension-owned frame. Only clips classified as speech reach transcription.
The old high-peak click gate, 150-ms onset delay and strict voiced-duration
requirement are bypassed while AI speech detection is enabled. Candidate
recording uses a lower energy threshold, preserving quieter speech. Long
candidate clips are checked at 12 seconds rather than silently discarded.
The existing Minimum speech slider applies only with AI detection OFF.

The local model requires at least three speech-positive 32-ms frames,
including two consecutive frames, and peak speech probability >= 0.65.
The audio band is widened to 80-7600 Hz while detection is enabled.
Model loading/classification errors are displayed; noisy audio is not
silently uploaded as a fallback. Stop/off during loading cancels the start.

TESTING
64 automated checks pass plus JavaScript syntax checks. Unlike the older
filter, testing executes the real bundled ONNX model and WASM runtime:
silence and synthetic keyboard clicks were rejected; locally generated
speech, and the same speech mixed with synthetic clicks, were accepted.
This does not prove behavior for every real voice, keyboard or Steam audio
layout. Live Chrome iframe/model loading and your friends' audio still
need testing. No actual voice/chat messages or downloads were submitted.

WHAT TO EXPECT
Startup may briefly say Loading local AI speech detector. During analysis
it says Checking speech locally. Keyboard-only clips should show Background
noise ignored rather than sending AI requests. Test by typing silently,
then speaking a normal sentence. This is speech detection, not source
separation: typing mixed with actual speech may remain audible. Television,
music with vocals and other actual speech may also be classified as speech.

All prior feature changes remain included, including replay prevention.
Electron and native Steam companion source are unchanged. Existing
experimental integration labels still apply. No settings/keys are cleared.
