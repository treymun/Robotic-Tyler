# ROBOTIC TYLER v2.41.1: Tidier Settings

## Changed
- **Settings has three more tabs, so the long tabs are much shorter.** The tabs are the same everywhere: in the desktop app, in Chrome and in Settings on the phone remote.
  - **Chat images** has the `!image` settings: styles and models, limits, who can ask, and the Venice key. They used to be at the bottom of AI & personality.
  - **Theme voices** has the theme voices and the character voices. They used to be at the bottom of Voice. In Chrome the tab says they're part of the desktop app.
  - **On this PC** (desktop app) has text replies, hearing and the voice, and images on this PC, with their installs. They used to sit in the middle of AI & personality, and only showed once you chose one of them. The tab is always there now, so you can install them first and choose them after.
- **Appearance shows two columns of theme cards beside the big preview.** One column left an empty gap, so the gallery is about half as long. The cards are a little smaller; the themes themselves are unchanged.
- **How long the tabs are now** (the desktop app at 1280 × 900, nothing installed):
  - AI & personality: about 4,600 → 2,900 pixels. It was longer still when the AI on this PC was in use.
  - Voice: 4,000 → 1,850 (theme voices moved out).
  - Appearance: 5,100 → 2,850.
  - On the phone, AI & personality went from 6,500 to 3,800.
- **Links that open Settings at a section open its tab:** from the panel, the phone remote, the setup guide, and the messages that say where something is set up. Those messages now say *Settings → On this PC*, *Settings → Chat images* and *Settings → Theme voices*.
- Each new tab has its own icon, drawn in every theme's style (pixel art in DOOM).
