# ROBOTIC TYLER v2.42.0: The voice supplement for the character voices

## New: improved character voices from the voice supplement (desktop app, optional)
- **5 of the 14 character voices that were weak, rough or not offered now measure closer to the real character, when you import the voice supplement: Coach and Seven (now *ready*), Kelvin (now *weak*, was an *approximation*), and Zoey and Venator (closer, still *weak*).**
- **Medic is offered now.** He keeps his v2.41 voice, which needs only the first pack. v2.41 couldn't test him reliably; the supplement's recordings of him make a reliable test possible, and he passes it as *weak*.
- **No one listened to these voices before this release.** You asked for the scores to decide: *"Just improve all of the ones that need it, I don't have time to listen to them myself before you publish."*
  - Each decision comes from a speaker model and a speech recogniser, with the rules of the v2.41 polish pass. The processor-time rule is the one change (see below).
- **Unchanged:** Scout, Soldier, Pyro, Demoman, Heavy, Sniper, Spy and Lash, and Medic's voice itself. Their supplement candidates didn't pass the rules, so they keep their v2.41 voice (details below). Pyro still isn't offered.
- **Importing it:** use the same **Settings → Theme voices → Import character voices** button. Give it the voice supplement's folder or `Robotic-Tyler-Complete-Voice-Supplement.zip`.
  - You can import it before or after the character voice pack. You can also give one folder that holds both packs, unpacked or as their ZIPs.
  - What's already imported stays.
  - Only the listed clips are read: 30 supplement clips (the ones the improved voices use), about 2.7 MB. Each is checked by its path, size, FLAC header and SHA-256.
- **Versions:** every character's v2.41 voice is now its **version 1**, which needs only the first pack. An improved character's **version 2** is the one it uses.
  - **Without the supplement**, an improved character speaks its version 1 and says so: *"Earlier version (v1): import the voice supplement for the improved version (v2)."*
    - The note appears in Settings (on its row and in the status line), on the panel, on the clip and on the phone remote.
    - The Settings status line also counts the improved voices that still need the supplement.
  - **Offered only in version 2:** no character is, in this release. If one ever is, it isn't offered without the supplement, and the reason is shown.
  - Tyler never switches silently.
- **Going back:**
  - **Use the earlier version**, under the character's note in Settings, goes back to version 1, remembered for that character.
  - **Use the improved version** goes forward again, like the built-in voices.

## The re-tune: before and after
- **How it was measured:**
  - On this project's 2-core test machine without a graphics card.
  - With **Qwen3-TTS 0.6B (Lightweight)** through the app's own voice worker.
  - Every candidate that passed on two seeds was then checked on **1.7B (Quality)**.
- **Candidates:** two for each character.
  - **C1:** a subset of the supplement's clips, with checked text.
  - **C2:** the v2.41 reference plus a few supplement clips.
  - **C3, Soldier only:** as you asked, a third candidate combines his v2.41 reference with the supplement's whole 4.5 s (both clips). It could only be tested against v2.41's four held-out recordings, because both new clips are in its reference.
- **The same test as before:** each version said the same four new sentences, with the same seeds, at the same level, without effects.
- **Held out:** the scores compare against real recordings that no reference uses: v2.41's held-out clips plus held-out clips from the supplement.
- **The baseline is reproduced exactly:** version 1 was made again in this session, and its takes came out byte-identical to v2.41's.
- **Likeness** is a speaker model's score (ECAPA), **not a listening verdict**.
- **Word accuracy** comes from a local Whisper small.en transcript.
- **The promotion rules (a candidate had to pass all of them):**
  - at least +0.03 likeness overall;
  - not less close on either group of held-out recordings;
  - closer on at least 3 of 4 sentences;
  - word accuracy at most 0.03 lower, and no sentence below 0.75;
  - all of that on two seeds;
  - not less close on 1.7B.
- **One change from v2.41: processor time is reported, not a rule.**
  - v2.41 rejected a candidate that took more than 1.25× the time. This time it's reported only.
  - **Why:** the task's rules didn't list it, and on this machine it's too noisy to decide by. The same unchanged voice (Seven's version 1) measured 7.7 and 6.4 s per second of speech on two seeds.
  - **This was decided after the first seed's scores were seen.**
  - **The cost:** a longer reference is slower on a processor. The five version-2 voices take 1.2–1.6× the time: Zoey 1.6×, Coach 1.5×, Seven and Kelvin 1.3×, Venator 1.2× (the mean of two seeds). Speed on an NVIDIA card wasn't measured.
- **Pyro and Medic** could become offered only through the same test plus the v2.41 offer bar: a likeness ratio of at least 0.15 on both seeds. The held-out recordings also had to agree with the character: his own reference had to score at least 0.45 against them.

## How it behaves
- **Without the supplement nothing gets worse.**
  - Every character that was offered in v2.41 speaks exactly as it did: version 1 has the same reference, text and model, and it produced byte-identical takes.
  - Medic is new; he needs only the first pack.
- **Caches:** each version has its own cache key, made from every clip's SHA-256, its text and its version number. Versions 1 and 2 never share a conditioning file.
- **The store:** an import from v2.41 moves to the new layout (one file per clip, named by its SHA-256) the first time it's read. You don't need to import again.
- **Limits:**
  - A clip is at most 4 MB, and one import at most 64 MB. Both packs' listed clips total about 14.7 MB.
  - A folder may hold at most four pack ZIPs (`Robotic-Tyler-*.zip`), each at most 2 GB.
  - A clip is kept only if its SHA-256 matches, whichever pack it's found in.
- **Everything else is as before:** Standard voice, Gemini Live, `!say` (including `!say tf2/scout TEXT`), the theme defaults and your own recordings.
