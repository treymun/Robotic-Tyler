ROBOTIC TYLER v2.3.23 - Chrome solo skipping and vote-to-kick

INSTALL
Close Steam web chat AND Tyler's YouTube player tabs. Replace ALL files in
your SAME unpacked browser-extension folder, including the new
steam-chat-main.js, steam-chat-client.js, moderation-core.js and moderation.js.
Keep neural. Reload the existing extension at chrome://extensions. Reopen
Steam chat, Connect & start and reopen/share a YouTube music tab as before.
Do not uninstall or change the loaded folder path; saved settings stay.

SOLO MUSIC SKIP
When the bot can confirm exactly one other member in its ACTIVE voice
channel, that actual person's music skip passes with one vote. Group voice
still needs two distinct voters. Voice membership is read fresh on each
skip; silent/muted people are still counted. An unrelated text-chat user
does not receive the solo exception. Unknown, changing, different-group or
unavailable voice membership keeps the two-vote rule. Keep the active voice
channel visible. This works independently of AI speech mode and does not
require moderator permissions. Stop/reset still require two different
accounts, including in solo sessions. All earlier spam protections remain.

VOTE-TO-KICK
New experimental Chrome moderation panel. Give the bot's Steam account
kick permission through the group's own role settings, check permissions
in Tyler's panel, and enable vote-to-kick for that group. It defaults off
for new groups; Tyler cannot promote itself.

!votekick NAME - call a vote; caller counts as first yes.
!kick yes / !kick no - one vote per identified eligible account.
!kick status / !kick cancel / !kick help.

Three different yes voters automatically cause a ONE-HOUR group-chat kick.
This is a group removal, not only voice disconnection. Owner and bot are
protected. Target and bot cannot vote. Ballots last 60 seconds; three no
votes reject early; five-minute cooldown between ballot starts.
The panel offers a member dropdown when names are ambiguous and can cancel
an open ballot. No IDs are typed in for targets or voters.

Checks before execution: current group, enabled setting, active extension
tab, emergency stop, Steam's kick permission, protected target, current
membership, three distinct yes voters and fresh vote messages. Old history
does not start fresh votes. Each ballot can execute only once; uncertain
or failed kicks are not retried automatically. Group change/disable/stop
cancels pending ballots. See MODERATION-GUIDE.md for complete setup/rules.
