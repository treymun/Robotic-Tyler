# Source artwork and credits

This add-on contains copied source files, faithful community drawings, new utility controls and small custom branding pieces. These classes are identified in the manifest. The new supporting vectors are not claimed to be official game or platform assets.

Source readmes/notices are included in `source-notices/`. Original graphical files are retained unchanged under `originals/`, with their file hashes and source/blob identifiers in `SOURCES.json`. ICO resources also have native-size PNG decodes for direct use.

The custom branding pieces reuse the earlier pack’s emblem geometry. Relevant prior Simple Icons, Papirus and Font Awesome license texts are included under `licenses/`. See Pack 1 for its complete source inventory.

FamFamFam Silk is by Mark James: http://www.famfamfam.com/lab/icons/silk/ — the original attribution notice is included. TF2 class vectors are credited to the TF2Stadium contributors under the upstream class-icon terms. Graphical game/platform files retain their original owners’ notices; a collection’s tooling license does not relicense all its images.
