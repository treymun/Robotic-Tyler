Where Tyler joins voice

Tyler can join Steam voice right in this app's Steam chat window, like the Chrome extension: no VB-CABLE, no Steam desktop app. Or the older way, through a virtual audio cable and the Steam desktop app.

Audio devices

Tyler talks into a virtual audio cable that Steam uses as its microphone, and listens to the device Steam plays your friends through.

Devices load when this page opens.
