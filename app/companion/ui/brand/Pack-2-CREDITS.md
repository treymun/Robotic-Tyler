# Pack 2 artwork: what ROBOTIC TYLER uses, and from where

ROBOTIC TYLER v2.30 uses a few original pieces from the owner's "Icon and Branding Pack 2" where
they are more faithful to the theme's source than what it had. Everything else in that pack stays
in the source tree only (assets/theme-icons-pack-2), unused and not shipped. The Tyler logos are
unchanged.

The artwork belongs to its owners, listed per theme. Each collection's own notice ships next to
this file (Pack-2-*.txt). Pack 1's icons and their licences: CREDITS.md and the other .txt files.
