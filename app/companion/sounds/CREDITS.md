Source identities below follow the upstream collections; extraction history is not independently certified.

Only selected audio inputs are included. Full game archives, firmware images, music libraries and unrelated test songs are not included.

## Newly authored audio

The iPod click-wheel/piezo set and the PAYDAY drill/cash-counter designs were synthesized for this pack. PAYDAY pager/countdown hybrid cues combine synthesis with a referenced source texture. No voice cloning was used.

Rhythmic rolls, countdown sequences, failure contours and sweeps are described by their operations and exact inputs in recipes.json. Preserve this distinction when integrating or describing the sounds.
