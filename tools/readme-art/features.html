Voice

Talks in your voice channel

Tyler joins the call on his own microphone and answers out loud, in the voice you pick.

  • Standard waits for a pause, writes a reply with your AI provider and speaks it.
  • Gemini Live answers in about a second and stops when someone talks over him.
  • Smooth and even: no crackle, no gap mid-reply, the same loudness every time.
  • He only hears the call he's in, after you confirm everyone knows.
Personality

He's your guy, your way

Start from a preset, then shape him with sliders or write him up in your own words.

  • Presets: Rowdy squad, Helpful coach, Chaos goblin, Chill wingman.
  • Roast level, reply length, speed and how talkative he is.
  • Preview a reply before you save. People in chat can't change his rules.
Room tools

Music, sounds and squad games

Everything is off until you switch it on in Tyler's panel, and friends use it from chat.

  • Music DJ: !play artist song, a fair queue and skip votes. YouTube's sound goes in untouched, in stereo, and dips while Tyler talks.
  • Soundboard: clip pads straight into the call, no API key needed.
  • Squad: trivia races, the arcade, game votes, party host and XP.

Desktop app

One app, no Chrome needed

Its own Steam chat window, with Tyler's panel docked beside the chat instead of on top of it.

  • Voice right in that window: no VB-CABLE, no Steam desktop app.
  • Drag the panel's edge to resize it, or float it over the chat.
  • Its own YouTube window for music and its own screen picker for commentary.
  • Keys encrypted by Windows. Updates itself.
Phone remote

Run Tyler from your phone

His panel on your phone, live and in your theme, with the theme's own extras. Nothing to install: the desktop app serves it on your home Wi-Fi.

  • Start and pause him, play clips, run the Music DJ, type in chat, run the full check.
  • Switch the theme for every Tyler window, right from the phone.
  • Paired with a code from Settings. It never sees your keys or the chat.

Long nights

He stays in the call

Tyler keeps himself in the call and on the chat, and tells you plainly when something needs you.

  • Stay connected: after 45 quiet minutes he hums once, softly, so Steam doesn't drop him. If Steam redraws the chat, he finds the same channel again.
  • Full check: chat, microphone, call, music, AI key, speech and a test beep in one pass.
  • Repeat protection: he notices when he's saying the same thing again.
