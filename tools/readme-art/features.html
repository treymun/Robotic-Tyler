Voice

Talks in your voice channel

Tyler joins the call on his own microphone and answers out loud, in the voice you pick.

  • Standard waits for a pause, writes a reply with your AI provider and speaks it.
  • Gemini Live answers in about a second and stops when someone talks over him.
  • He only hears the call he's in, after you confirm everyone knows.
Personality

He's your guy, your way

Start from a preset, then shape him with sliders or write him up in your own words.

  • Presets: Rowdy squad, Helpful coach, Chaos goblin, Chill wingman.
  • Roast level, reply length, speed and how talkative he is.
  • Preview a reply before you save. People in chat can't change his rules.
Room tools

Music, sounds and squad games

Everything is off until you switch it on in Tyler's panel, and friends use it from chat.

  • Music DJ: !play artist song, a fair queue and skip votes.
  • Soundboard: clip pads straight into the call, no API key needed.
  • Squad: trivia races, the arcade, game votes, party host and XP.

Desktop app

One app, no Chrome needed

Its own Steam chat window with Tyler's panel, the same as in Chrome.

  • Voice right in that window: no VB-CABLE, no Steam desktop app.
  • Its own YouTube window for music and its own screen picker for commentary.
  • Keys encrypted by Windows. Updates itself.
